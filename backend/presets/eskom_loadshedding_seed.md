# Eskom Loadshedding Announcement — Simulation Seed Document

## Official Announcement (Simulated)

**Eskom Media Statement — 22 July 2026**

Eskom regrettably informs the public that loadshedding will resume effective
immediately at Stage 3. This follows multiple generation unit failures at
Kusile Power Station (Units 1 and 3 tripped within 12 hours of each other),
Medupi Power Station (Unit 4 on unplanned outage due to boiler tube leak),
and Kriel Power Station (two units taken offline for emergency repairs).

Current unplanned outages stand at 14,500 MW, with planned maintenance at
6,200 MW. Emergency reserves (diesel-fired open cycle gas turbines and
pumped storage) have been depleted to critically low levels after sustained
use over the past week to keep the system running without loadshedding.

The return of loadshedding comes after a 78-day period of uninterrupted
power supply — the longest stretch without loadshedding since 2022.

Stage 3 loadshedding means most South Africans will experience approximately
4 hours of power cuts, three times per day, on a rotational schedule.

Eskom is working urgently to return units to service. Estimated recovery
time for Kusile Unit 1 is 48-72 hours. However, if additional units trip,
higher stages of loadshedding may be implemented.

"We have turned the corner on our energy crisis. For the first time in over
a decade, South Africans are enjoying uninterrupted power. Our Energy Action
Plan is working. We have added over 4,000 MW of new capacity to the grid
through renewables, battery storage, and restored Eskom units. Loadshedding
is behind us."

"The days of loadshedding are over. Our reforms are delivering results. We
are confident that South Africa will not see loadshedding again this winter."

## Key Background Facts

1. **15+ years of loadshedding** — South Africans have experienced intermittent
   loadshedding since 2007, with the worst period being 2022-2023 (over 200
   days of loadshedding in 2023 alone).

2. **Economic cost** — The South African Reserve Bank estimates loadshedding
   costs the economy approximately R900 million per day at Stage 3, and up to
   R3 billion per day at Stage 6.

3. **Eskom debt** — Eskom carries approximately R400 billion in debt. The
   government has assumed R254 billion of this debt in multiple bailouts.

4. **Generation capacity** — Eskom's nominal capacity is ~48,000 MW, but
   Energy Availability Factor (EAF) has declined from 70% in 2021 to below
   55% in 2026 due to aging infrastructure and maintenance backlogs.

5. **Renewable growth** — Private renewable energy projects have added over
   4,000 MW since 2022, but grid connection bottlenecks and transmission
   constraints limit their impact.

6. **Solar adoption** — South Africa has seen explosive growth in rooftop
   solar, with an estimated 6,000+ MW of private rooftop solar installed by
   2026, primarily in wealthy and middle-income areas.

7. **EskomSePush app** — The most-downloaded utility app in South Africa,
   with over 8 million active users. It sends push notifications about
   loadshedding schedules and has become a cultural touchpoint.

8. **Political landscape** — The ANC lost its absolute majority in the 2024
   elections, forming a Government of National Unity (GNU) with the DA and
   smaller parties. Energy policy is a key fault line in the coalition.

9. **Cross-border impacts** — South Africa imports ~1,500 MW from Mozambique's
   Cahora Bassa dam. Loadshedding reduces domestic demand but also impacts
   power export agreements.

10. **Crime and safety** — Studies show correlation between loadshedding and
    increased crime rates, particularly burglary and cable theft. Traffic
    congestion increases due to non-functional traffic lights.

## Political Party Positions

**ANC (African National Congress)**
- Points to 78-day uninterrupted period as evidence of progress
- Blames legacy issues and "decades of underinvestment" under previous administrations
- Emphasizes Energy Action Plan achievements
- Calls for patience and national unity

**DA (Democratic Alliance)**
- Calls for immediate privatization of Eskom generation
- Accuses ANC of "lying to the public" about the end of loadshedding
- Demands parliamentary inquiry into Eskom maintenance failures
- Proposes accelerated independent power producer (IPP) procurement

**EFF (Economic Fighters)**
- Calls for nationalization of private renewable energy installations
- Blames "white monopoly capital" for sabotaging Eskom
- Demands free electricity for all households
- Organizes protest marches to Eskom headquarters

**ActionSA**
- Proposes splitting Eskom into generation, transmission, and distribution
- Calls for criminal investigation of Eskom executives
- Demands compensation for small businesses affected by loadshedding

## Economic Impact Data

- **Small businesses**: 40% report losing inventory (refrigeration) during
  Stage 3 loadshedding. Average daily cost: R2,500-R5,000 per SME.
- **Restaurants**: 60% report reduced operating hours; 25% report staff
  layoffs during extended loadshedding periods.
- **Taxi industry**: Increased fuel costs due to traffic congestion from
  non-functional traffic lights. Estimated R15,000 additional per taxi per
  month during loadshedding.
- **Hospitals**: Backup generator costs average R50,000-R200,000 per month
  per facility during loadshedding. Rural clinics often lack backup power.
- **Mining sector**: Stage 3+ loadshedding reduces sector output by 10-15%,
  costing an estimated R600 million per day.
- **Solar market**: Rooftop solar inquiries increase 300-500% during
  loadshedding announcements. Average payback period: 5-7 years.

## Social Media Context

- **Twitter/X**: Primary platform for real-time reactions. Hashtags like
  #Loadshedding, #Eskom, and #EskomSePush trend within minutes of
  announcements. Political discourse is intense.
- **Reddit (r/southafrica)**: Longer-form analysis, schedule sharing, solar
  installation tips, dark humour memes, and political debate. Community
  has ~250,000 subscribers.
- **Cultural phenomena**: South Africans have developed a unique culture
  around loadshedding — dark humour, "EskomSePush check" rituals, memes
  comparing loadshedding stages to personal struggles, and collective
  coping mechanisms.
- **EskomSePush notifications**: The push notification sound has become
  a Pavlovian trigger for anxiety and planning across the country.

## Neighbouring Country Impact

- **Zimbabwe**: Already experiences 18+ hour daily power cuts. SA
  loadshedding reduces potential imports. Zimbabwean social media shows
  sympathy mixed with "welcome to our world" sentiment.
- **Mozambique**: Cahora Bassa exports to SA; reduced demand affects
  revenue. Mozambican citizens question why exported power isn't used
  domestically.
- **Namibia/Botswana**: Import power from SA; reduced supply affects
  border regions and mining operations.
